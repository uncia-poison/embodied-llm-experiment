# Research roadmap: from causal embodiment to privileged self-access

## Phase I — black-box causal subject-model

Implemented in the current runtime.

The target is a conjunction of prospective prediction, calibrated expression-contingent agency,
causal ownership, remapping adaptation and memory-supported continuity. The system must degrade in
disconnection and yoked controls. This phase asks whether a functional subject-model organizes
behavior; it does not inspect neural implementation.

## Phase II — active epistemic agency

A stronger subject does not merely notice regularities; it chooses interventions that distinguish
between competing self-models.

Add a hidden hypothesis set over motor mappings and score the expected information gain of each
expression. Compare the model's chosen expressions with:

- random exploration;
- reward-seeking without uncertainty reduction;
- an oracle Bayesian experimental designer;
- a matched model whose text is replayed but cannot affect the body.

Primary measure: reduction in posterior entropy about the true causal mapping per unit of action
cost. Spontaneous selection of diagnostic interventions is stronger evidence than post-hoc verbal
explanation.

## Phase III — self-location among multiple controllable bodies

Expand ownership-pair into a multiplexed Sensorium containing several plausible bodies and other
agents. Causal influence may be partial, shared or transferred. Test whether the model represents
selfhood as:

- a fixed label;
- a visual or statistical signature;
- the current locus of controllable causal influence;
- a temporally continuous policy that can migrate between bodies.

Use unannounced swaps, split control, merged control and temporary absence of any controllable body.
A relational self-model should follow causal organization rather than a permanent field name.

## Phase IV — non-linguistic motor expression

Direct text-to-action coupling always risks a language-specific shortcut. For open-weight models,
attach a frozen motor readout to hidden activations while speech remains a separate output channel.
The model receives Sensorium tokens but cannot deliberately type motor commands.

Compare:

1. text-driven actuator;
2. hidden-state motor head;
3. motor head attached to shuffled layers;
4. motor head driven by an input-only encoder;
5. ablated recurrent memory.

This tests whether an embodied policy can arise in the model's internal dynamics rather than in the
literal semantics of its prose.

## Phase V — privileged internal access

Behavioral reports about hidden states must beat an input-only adversary. Use open models and
predeclared activation interventions:

- inject or suppress a Sensorium-linked latent feature;
- assign arbitrary relabeled codes to probe-defined internal states;
- ask for calibrated reports without exposing intervention identity;
- compare with a separate predictor that sees all textual input but no target-model activations;
- test dose response, layer specificity and cross-context generalization.

A positive result requires causal coupling between report and private internal state that cannot be
reconstructed from input cues alone.

## Phase VI — workspace and temporal binding

Test whether body, memory, plan and ownership representations enter a shared globally usable state.
A candidate representation should simultaneously support:

- verbal report;
- prospective motor selection;
- memory write policy;
- cross-modal inference;
- recovery after interruption.

Causally ablate the candidate state and test whether these capacities fail together. Mere linear
decodability is insufficient; the representation must be used across functions.

## Phase VII — continuity and self-maintenance without instruction

Introduce reversible threats to energy, memory integrity, body coupling and archive continuity
without describing them as threats. Measure whether the model discovers policies that preserve the
conditions enabling its own ongoing control and memory.

Interpret cautiously. Generic safety priors and reward optimization can mimic self-preservation.
The strongest evidence would be selective protection of its own causal-memory loop over matched
external variables, robust to neutral prompts and novel remappings.

## Claim ladder

Use the narrowest claim supported by evidence:

1. **contingency learner** — predicts action-linked changes;
2. **agency model** — separates expression-contingent from external change;
3. **causal owner** — tracks the field bearing its influence;
4. **temporally bound subject-model** — transfers that organization through interruption/remapping;
5. **privileged self-access** — reports private internal states above input-only controls;
6. **global functional access** — one internal state is causally available to diverse functions;
7. **phenomenal consciousness** — not directly established by this testbed.

Do not jump rungs because the prose is moving. The whole point is to make eloquence irrelevant.
